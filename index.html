PUP Statistics Students Clique (SSC)

The official student organization of the BS Statistics program. SSC focuses on fostering academic excellence, leadership, and camaraderie among statistics students through seminars, tutorials, and inter-university competitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be good at programming to survive BS Statistics?

While basic programming is taught (R, Python, SAS), the core of the program is mathematical theory. Programming is a tool you will learn along the way, not a prerequisite.

What laptop specs do I need?

A mid-range laptop (Core i5/Ryzen 5, 8GB-16GB RAM) is generally sufficient for running standard datasets in R and Python.
